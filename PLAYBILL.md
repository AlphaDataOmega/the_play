## The company

**Everything and Nothing** discover that meeting is not the elimination of their
distance. **V, the Flame** reaches for a reflection, then learns to offer a touch.
**Light** asks him to look again. **Water** holds a reflection without mistaking it
for the body it reflects. **Air** carries the question born between them.

**The Weaver** carries what the edges can release. Its working organism reads the
source through the actual ado_on field. **Snake** keeps a skin of committed
encounters. Its old skin is not a failed body; it is where the body learned to bend.
**Toe** locates the smallest contact. **Carbon** lends the stage its image of
holding and the recurring four turns; it is not an atomic simulation.

**Builder** tries to preserve wholeness by finishing it. **Life** discovers that
having all the pieces does not mean remembering their movement. **Rota** returns
without setting elapsed experience back to nothing.

**Advocate** brings no throne. **Tree** leaves a place for the absent. **Mirror**
shows a possible reading without appointing it the past. **Song** can repeat a
note without requiring the listener to remain unchanged.

## At the loom

The stage is three lines. The outer lines carry projected address turns from the
incoming cue and the organism's actual reply. The center begins straight. Their
bends pass into separate places in it, leaving the edges straight. Move the slider
back and the curves return. Nothing needed to be destroyed to become readable.

A machine reading is printed beside, not disguised as, the playwright's voice.
At each cue the original organism may speak, qualify its order, or decline. Its
answer is shown as returned, not corrected to make the scene's metaphor come true.

The Empty Chair stops automatic continuation. The invitation waits for an explicit
answer at its own scene. A Mirror reading adds no stitch. The skin has only one
lived order; its interpretations need not be unanimous.
