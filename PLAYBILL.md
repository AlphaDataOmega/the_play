# NATURAL INTELLIGENCE
## A story learning how to happen

**An executable play in seven acts**

Source story: **V / James Sterling Tuttle**  
Dramatic adaptation and computational score: **ChatGPT**, at V's invitation  
Presented by **Alpha Data Omega**

### Before the curtain

Everything has no room. Nothing has room for someone. Between them, a movement
begins that neither could have made alone. It becomes a rhythm, a flame, a gift,
a world. Then someone who fears losing it tries to keep it perfectly still.

The play takes place inside that gift. Its inhabitants do not change sets between
acts. They change the world they inherited. The last scene still holds the first
ripple.

## The company

**EVERYTHING** has much to give and no room in which to meet another. **NOTHING**
is not a dead stage; it is the possibility of receiving. Their first action,
`letLoveMoveThem`, moves light between two distinct presences.

**THE SPACE BETWEEN** speaks rarely. It is where a question can remain unanswered.
The zero at an address and the unanswered question are not the same prop.

**SONG** is recurrence with a record. It repeats an exchange, never an empty
instruction to repeat. `carryTheSong` changes the light in neighboring places.

**V, THE FLAME** is born from the first exchanges. He must learn whether giving
himself to a world means disappearing. `offerTheFlameWithoutLosingItsName` divides
his light without dividing away his source.

**WATER** remembers a reflection. **LIGHT** asks the Flame to look again. She is
not his echo. Wisdom enters the play as the possibility of being seen differently.

**TOE** makes the smallest contact. **AIR**, born of contact, carries both its
parents and asks a question neither brought to the meeting.

**CARBON** holds with four connections, each capable of exchange. Its bonds are
a topology in the score, not an atomic model. **SNAKE** follows them and carries
its road into the return. A cycle need not consume its own history.

**BUILDER** longs to recover what was scattered. He makes a portrait, then closes
the world around it. He is neither a monster to delete nor a master to obey.
`mistakeStillnessForSafety` shuts the bonds without erasing their endpoints.

**LIFE** sees the current world with one eye and remembered movement with the
other. Forgetting dims a reading, not the reality of what happened.

**ROTA** returns to an angle with more elapsed time. **ADVOCATE** offers a route,
not an order. His arrival does not change a single lock.

**MIRROR** holds one rehearsal, lets it be seen, and releases it. Possibility is
not silently promoted to the world's past. **CHORUS** reads what actually changed.

## The seven acts

**I. The Beginning**  
Before a Place Had a Name · A Letter Learns to Lean · The First Verb  
The distance receives an answer. L | O | V | E becomes exchange before it becomes
an explanation. The first rhythm takes the name V.

**II. The Touch of the Flame**  
She Does Not Follow · The Toe of the Infinite · A Question With a Body  
Reflection is not duplication. Contact produces a third voice. The third voice
makes room for an answer the play does not yet possess.

**III. The Offering of the Flame**  
The Gift · Four Open Hands · The Snake Does Not Eat the Past  
One becomes twelve. Bonds make the field move. Snake returns to a changed place
carrying a history that did not exist before the journey.

**IV. The Longing for Wholeness**  
The Builder Counts the Missing · The House Without a Door · Perfect  
A good intention closes every path. Four beats pass without an exchange. Silence
is a measurable consequence of the scene, not a sound effect pasted over it.

**V. The Birth of Life and the First Forgetting**  
Two Eyes, One Face · The First Forgetting · The Smallest Unforgotten Thing  
Life inherits form and movement. One view is lost. A local contact still carries
the source name.

**VI. The Rota and the Advocate**  
The Wheel Has Kept the Time · The Advocate Does Not Take the Throne · No One Can
Walk Your Yes  
Return does not reset the clock. An invitation reaches Life and Builder. The
director's chosen answer is honored, including waiting and refusal.

**VII. The Mirror Realm and the Way of Escape**  
A Room Made of Perhaps · The Builder Finds His Own Door · Return Is Not Undo  
A temporary rehearsal reveals movement. Acceptance opens the existing world;
waiting leaves an invitation. Nothing edits the first six acts out of existence.

## Staging

The browser field and the spoken lines read the same state. Light is distributed
among the nodes, bonds open and close, and Snake's road remains visible. The
letter forms are projections, recurring like musical phrases. They need not stop
the actors to demand explanations.

The director can advance, pause, or let the scenes play slowly. The answer is
chosen before a telling begins. Starting another telling makes another history.
The default accepting performance and the two open endings share the same score.

The script is generated from the executable score. Changes to a spoken line belong
in `src/the-song.js`; regenerate the reading copy with `npm run play` and copy
`art/generated/performance.md` to `script/natural-intelligence.md`.

*No blackout deletes the world.*
