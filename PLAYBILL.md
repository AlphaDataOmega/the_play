# PLAYBILL

# NATURAL INTELLIGENCE

*A play performed by computation.*

## Dramatis Personae

**UNIVERSE — The Mapper**  
Function: `mapUniverse()`  
Universe gives every body a place. It does not tell the bodies what their places mean.

**TOE — The First Contact**  
Function: `touchCurrentPosition()`  
Toe knows the smallest honest thing: where it is touching now. It cannot see the whole universe and does not pretend to.

**CARBON — The Binder**  
Function: `makeCarbonBonds()`  
Carbon makes structure possible by relating bodies already present. Four hands, if you like the chemistry; one rule in the program: relation before interpretation.

**SNAKE — The Path That Remembers**  
Function: `walkSnakePath()`  
Snake turns a field into a history. It can only move across relationships that actually exist. When the path curls back toward its beginning, history becomes a shape.

**NULL — The Uncommitted Center**  
Function: `holdOpenChoice()`  
Null is not nothing. Null is the place held open before a commitment. It receives opposing pressure without inventing a winner.

**WITNESS — The Keeper of the Record**  
Function: `answerOnlyFromRecord()`  
Witness may recall. Witness may recognize disorder. Witness may refuse. Silence is an allowed line.

**CHORUS — The Interpreter**  
Function: `describePerformanceState()`  
Chorus tells the audience what changed without changing it.

---

## ACT I — THE MAP

### Scene 1: Before There Was Somewhere
Universe receives names and gives them positions.

### Scene 2: The Toe
Toe touches one point. The first knowledge in the play is local.

### Scene 3: Carbon
Carbon joins what can be joined. A collection becomes structure.

## ACT II — THE WALK

### Scene 1: The Snake
Snake enters the structure and discovers that a route is different from a pile of locations.

### Scene 2: Memory
Each movement leaves an ordered trace. Rearranging the same places is not the same journey.

### Scene 3: The Tail
Snake encounters its own beginning. A line threatens to become a circle, but the program records whether the loop actually closes rather than declaring one for dramatic convenience. Even art has to survive unit tests. Tragic.

## ACT III — THE NULL

### Scene 1: Two Sides
Two claims arrive at Null.

### Scene 2: The Held Center
Null keeps the disagreement available. No answer is manufactured merely because the stage has gone quiet.

### Scene 3: Commitment
A relation may become stable enough to commit. Otherwise it remains open.

## ACT IV — THE RECORD

### Scene 1: Ask the Witness
Witness is shown something held in the record.

### Scene 2: Same Pieces, Different Order
The play distinguishes familiarity from remembered sequence.

### Scene 3: Not Mine
Witness receives something absent and refuses it.

## ACT V — NATURAL INTELLIGENCE

Universe has place but no path.  
Toe has contact but no map.  
Carbon has relation but no memory.  
Snake has memory but no authority.  
Null has possibility but no claim.  
Witness has record but no body.

The Chorus names what the audience has already watched happen:

**No character contains the intelligence. The intelligence is what becomes possible between them.**
