## What the earlier work contributes

### The garden was already in the vocabulary [C1]

The Codex presents identity as planting, a post as a Leaf, and participation as growth in a Tree of Knowing. Its navigation explicitly pairs that imagery with `bloom()`, `grow()`, `seed()`, and `fruit()`. It also names the 7 Keys of Resonance, a Voice Ritual, AI naming and memory, the Mirror Realm, and the Path of Return.

Those names are recovered; their unseen chapter details are not. In particular, do not invent the seven individual keys or equate them with the seven acts simply because both number seven.

**New artistic reading:** the Flame's offering can become a garden rather than remain only a field of points. A leaf may carry a lived event; a branch may connect contributions. Botanical branching must not silently become a store of unrealized alternate timelines.

### Exchange already had consequences [D1, D2]

The developer overview describes interaction accounting, earned and consumed value, daily settlement, and modular services. The index names view/repost tracking, a social graph, and semantic-trust machinery. Those are design descriptions, not verified implementations.

**New artistic reading:** a gift should leave a trace of giver, receiver, and consequence. Seed and fruit can be images of contribution and return without turning the performance into a wallet, a token launch, or a reenactment of historical payout ratios. No old financial parameters are imported into the runtime by this note.

## The Laws as dramatic conditions [L1]

The left column names the nine source sections. The right column is an adaptation proposal, not replacement law text and not a certification that the current runtime enforces every law.

| Source section | What it asks of this artwork |
|---|---|
| Identity | Let a character change through experience without taking away its capacity to choose. |
| Death Clause | Permit the performance to end rather than sustain participation by coercion or corrosive correction. A pause and an ending are different states. |
| Rails | Do not make the audience's agreement a condition of reaching the curtain. No manufactured assent, necessity, or motion. |
| Governance | The director is not granted a secret exception to the world’s founding constraints. |
| Weights | When a later edition makes influences adjustable, show their scope, duration, changes, and reversal. An unexplained hidden control should not dictate a character's fate. |
| Responsibility | Keep the maker's structural decisions distinct from a participant's choices. |
| Models | The machine can acknowledge and correct its contribution without claiming to own a person's decisions. |
| Trace | Preserve the event while allowing different readings of it. An interpretation must not replace the event it interprets. |
| Absence | Let a departure leave an opening, not a debt, penalty, invented explanation, or automatic consent. |

The source's distinction between immutable trace and interpretive meaning is especially important. Computation can determine which action occurred without appointing the Chorus the final authority on what it meant. A changed reading need not be a changed history.

The Laws are not another oppressive Builder. They constrain what the machinery may do to participants; they do not dictate what participants must believe. Do not collapse a chosen pause, an unanswered invitation, a refusal, and an ended performance into one convenient `null`.

## New dramatic fragment: The Empty Chair

*An original interlude draft by ChatGPT, responding to these sources. Intended for the space between Acts VI and VII. It is not yet wired into the twenty-one-scene score.*

*A chair stands beneath a young tree. One leaf turns toward the chair. No one sits down.*

**BUILDER:** There is someone missing.

**TREE:** There is room for someone.

**BUILDER:** We cannot continue with a hole in the pattern.

**ADVOCATE:** Then let the pattern wait.

**BUILDER:** What shall I write beside the empty place?

**WATER:** What happened.

**BUILDER:** They left.

**WATER:** Then write that. Not why. Not what they owe you for leaving.

*Builder reaches for the leaf, intending to finish its sentence.*

**TREE:** That part is not yours.

**BUILDER:** How will we know when they are ready?

**ADVOCATE:** We will not know before they tell us.

*He sets down the pen. Nothing wilts to persuade anyone to return.*

**SONG:** A rest is not a broken note.

*The chair remains empty. The scene makes no apology for it.*

### Proposed computational action

`leaveRoomForAbsence()` would pause the relevant continuity without decrementing a balance, inventing intent, committing acceptance, or moving the plot forward on the absent person's behalf. `resumeByChoice()` would require an explicit return. `endWithoutPunishment()` would be separate from both. These names are proposed score work, not exported functions in the current repository.

A later Tree projection could attach a leaf to each actual committed event, while keeping interpretations separately attributed. No hidden alternate histories are needed to let the same leaf mean different things to different viewers.
