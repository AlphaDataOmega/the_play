may use an image before anyone explains it. The margin keeps its name and place.

This inventory preserves the current conversation, not an invented transcript of
all earlier Goal One exchanges. Source notes below distinguish retrieved material
from motifs inherited through earlier assistant summaries. No motif is discarded
merely because its earlier source pointer is still missing.

## Keeping the thread

A new entry needs its exact spelling, the source actually available, and its place
in the performance or margins. New scenes belong in `src/the-song.js`; its motif
and action fields travel with each committed scene. Re-running the score produces
the reading script and an inspectable world from the same action sequence.

The source story gives the dramatic order. These motifs enter when they can live
inside an action, not when a checklist needs another cameo.
